## Notes and Caveats

* **System Divisions:** Only some systems divide the sky into 12. The Babylonian to Greek to Indian zodiac lineage and the Chinese Jupiter stations do so. Most others (Roman, Islamic, Hebrew, Ethiopian, Aztec, etc.) are calendar-month systems tied to the Moon or Sun.


* **Babylonian Influence:** Hebrew, Syriac, Assyrian, Old Persian, and Turkish month names derive from or parallel Babylonian ones. In the MUL.APIN scheme the "ideal" year is 12 months of 30 days, with the equinoxes on the 15th of months I and VII.


* **Egyptian Lineage:** Egyptian to Coptic to Ethiopian is one continuous descent of the 12 x 30 + 5 structure; the names changed, the structure did not.


* **Mesoamerican Systems:** Aztec and Maya have 18 twenty-day months, not 12; they are included because of the shared question of how cultures divided the year.


* **Celtic Dating:** Sources differ on whether the Coligny tablet is 1st c. BCE or late 2nd c. CE, and on whether Samonios falls near May or November.


* **Old Persian Records:** A few Old Persian names (months V and VI) are not securely attested. Treat that entry as partial.


* **Layered Naming:** Chinese and Hindu month names have several layers (numbered, classical, seasonal). The table shows the most commonly cited layers.
